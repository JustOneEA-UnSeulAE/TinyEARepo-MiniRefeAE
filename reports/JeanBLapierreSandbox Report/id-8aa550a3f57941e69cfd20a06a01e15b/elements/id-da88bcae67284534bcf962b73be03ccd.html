Evidence-based and time-bound ()
• Evidence-based and time-bound: Certification relies on concrete, verifiable evidence that demonstrates the quality and compliance of data assets. This means that assessments will be based on data quality metrics, documented processes, and validation results, ensuring that certification is grounded in factual information. Moreover, the certification process is time-bound, with periodic reviews and renewals to ensure that data remains relevant and in compliance with evolving standards and regulations.
