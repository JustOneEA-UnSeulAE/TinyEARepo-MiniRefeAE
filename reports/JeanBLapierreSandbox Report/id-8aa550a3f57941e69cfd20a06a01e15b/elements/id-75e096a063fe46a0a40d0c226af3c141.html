P2: Minimize investment and dependency to legacy applications​ ()
Principle 2: Minimize investment and dependency to legacy applications​ Statement: BDM must minimize investment in and modifications to legacy systems, especially those slated for decommissioning, to prevent increased complexity, risk, and technical debt. Rationale: Modernization efforts prioritize long-term strategic value by minimizing investment in legacy systems—defined as those slated for retirement in the Target State Architecture—which are often outdated and misaligned with enterprise goals. Continued investment risks operational disruptions, increased maintenance costs, and technical debt, diverting resources from scalable, innovative solutions. By focusing on sustainable transformation, it supports cost efficiency, agility, and alignment with the enterprise’s future vision. Implications: • The target state will maintain a single system of record; during transition phases, any temporary duplication must be actively managed, tracked, and reconciled.​ • Transactions must be processed in either the new or legacy system, not both, unless justified, documented, and approved as part of a formal transitional strategy.​ • Data cleansing or changes to support migration must occur outside legacy system modifications, adhering to defined data governance principles.​ • Where coexistence with legacy systems is required, a defined strategy (e.g., manual interventions) must be documented to ensure operational continuity.​ • Any remaining dependencies on legacy systems must include an exit plan aligned with the technology roadmap to support decommissioning and reduce technical debt.
