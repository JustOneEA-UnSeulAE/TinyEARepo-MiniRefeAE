Ideal Future-State Steward Profile
If ESDC were staffing a lead position responsible for Government-wide Disability Type Reference Data Stewardship, the strongest candidate would likely possess:
• Disability policy and accessibility expertise
• Enterprise data governance experience
• Metadata and reference data management knowledge
• Public-sector service delivery experience
• Statistical classification familiarity
• Privacy and ethical data management expertise
• Intergovernmental stakeholder management capability
• Enterprise architecture literacy
In practical terms, the role resembles a hybrid of a Chief Data Steward, Accessibility Policy Lead, and National Standards Manager, rather than a traditional data administrator. The steward's primary responsibility is not maintaining codes, but ensuring that disability-related data remains authoritative, interoperable, inclusive, privacy-conscious, operationally useful, and trusted across Canada’s disability ecosystem.
