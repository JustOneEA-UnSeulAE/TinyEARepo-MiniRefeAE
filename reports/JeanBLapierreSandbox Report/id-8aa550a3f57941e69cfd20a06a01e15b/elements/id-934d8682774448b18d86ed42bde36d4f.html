2.4 A business process concept model specifies data context.
Data is collected for a purpose. Concept process models show authorities, activities and the data used by, and created by, activities. Process models provide data context and describe the role of representations in the business. Process and activity related terminology may only appear on process models, and yet may be very important to understanding the conceptual and logical models. A conceptual process model is not the result of business process engineering; it forms a statement of requirement for that process by describing the essence of what the business is doing independently of any technology.
Rationale
• Data exists to support business activities.
• Process context explains why and how data is created.
Architecture Implications
• Link data concepts to authorities and activities.
• Keep process terminology out of data models.
• Use process models to contextualize representations.
