WHAT IS CERTIFICATION? ()
Data Certification Certification of enterprise data assets is a rigorous process designed to enhance data quality and governance, ensuring that data is reliable and aligned with business needs. The following principles define what certification is: • Evidence-based and time-bound: Certification relies on concrete, verifiable evidence that demonstrates the quality and compliance of data assets. This means that assessments will be based on data quality metrics, documented processes, and validation results, ensuring that certification is grounded in factual information. Moreover, the certification process is time-bound, with periodic reviews and renewals to ensure that data remains relevant and in compliance with evolving standards and regulations. • Risk-aware and proportional: Effective certification takes into consideration the varying levels of risk associated with different data assets. By adopting a risk-aware approach, organizations can prioritize which data requires more stringent controls and governance measures. This proportionality ensures that resources are allocated effectively, allowing for a more focused and efficient certification process that aligns with the organization's risk appetite. • Repeatable and auditable: A foundational aspect of certification is that it must be repeatable and subject to audit. This means that the processes and criteria used for certification should be well-documented and standardized, allowing for consistent application across different data assets. An auditable certification process also facilitates transparency and accountability, making it easier for stakeholders to verify the integrity of the data and the certification process itself. • Business-backed decision: Certification is not just a technical exercise; it requires the backing of business stakeholders who understand the strategic importance of data. Decisions regarding certification should be grounded in business objectives and the practical needs of users. This alignment ensures that the certification process is relevant and that the certified data can effectively support decision-making and drive business outcomes.
