P12: Just-in-Time & Just-Enough Architecture ()
Principle 12: Just-in-Time & Just-Enough Architecture Statement: BDM will prioritize making architecture decisions just in time for when they are needed, and with a level of detail that is just enough to meet requirements and not slow down projects.​ Rationale: Agile methodologies require that decisions be made at the right time. If decisions are made too early, technologies and circumstances may change by the time implementation is set to begin, and decisions made too late will slow down projects.​ For architecture, just enough is ideal. If architecture is too detailed, it falls back to the waterfall method and slows down projects. If not enough, consistency and reuse are lost. Implications: • BDM project teams will work to identify when decisions will need to be made to ensure decisions are made on-time, but not too early.​ • Project teams will manage and keep track of decisions for the future and have a plan in place for when it is time to proceed with those decisions.​ • Product owners and architects will participate in ongoing collaboration as sometimes design decisions by product owners can trigger architecture changes.​ • BDM will respect the schedules of projects as they are, with the understanding that decisions will be made if and when the time is right.
