ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES ()
5. Roles & Responsibilities The certification process involves various roles that each play a crucial part in ensuring the effectiveness and integrity of enterprise data. Clear delineation of responsibilities is essential for achieving successful certification outcomes and maintaining data governance. Business Subject Matter Expert (BSME) o Accountable for certification outcome: The Business Subject Matter Expert leads the certification process for the specific product and is ultimately responsible for the insights and interpretations the report provides. This role entails a thorough understanding of the data's intended use within the business, granularity, depth, and the criteria for fitness-for-use. o Accepts residual risk: The Business Subject Matter Expert acknowledges and accepts any residual risks associated with the data assets they oversee(identified and documented during the process implementation). This involves understanding the limitations of the data and the implications of potential inaccuracies or misuses in decision-making. Lead Data Stewards o Ensures enforcement, manages operations, provides direction and guidance: Lead Data Stewards oversee the implementation of data certification standards and practices within their domain. They manage daily operations related to data stewardship, ensuring that all certification-related activities are compliant with defined policies and procedures. Additionally, they provide support and guidance to Data Stewards, helping to harmonize practices across teams. Data Steward o Ensures metadata, lineage, and quality completeness: Data Stewards are responsible for maintaining comprehensive metadata and ensuring the accuracy of data lineage. They play a vital role in documenting the origin and movement of data throughout their lifecycle, which is essential for transparency and accountability in the certification process. o Coordinates certification activities: Data Stewards facilitates and coordinate the various activities involved in the certification process. This includes organizing assessments, gathering necessary documentation, and collaborating with relevant stakeholders to ensure a smooth certification workflow. Data Custodian: o Implement pipelines and transformations: Data Custodians are tasked with the technical aspects of data management, including the implementation of data pipelines and transformations. They ensure that data is processed and stored according to established standards, maintaining the integrity and accessibility of data assets for certification purposes. Data Trustee (Governance Committee): o Final certification authority: The Governance Committee serves as the highest authority in the certification process, making final decisions regarding the certification of data assets. This committee evaluates certification applications, reviews evidence and findings, and ensures that all certifications align with organizational policies and regulatory requirements. Additionally, the committee is responsible for timely governance meetings and decision-making, as well as providing strategic oversight and direction for certification activities. Privacy & Security o Validate regulatory and control compliance (External to certification team): The Privacy & Security team is responsible for ensuring that data assets meet all regulatory and compliance standards related to privacy and security. They conduct assessments to validate adherence to relevant laws and organizational controls, playing a critical role in safeguarding sensitive information and building trust in the certification process.
