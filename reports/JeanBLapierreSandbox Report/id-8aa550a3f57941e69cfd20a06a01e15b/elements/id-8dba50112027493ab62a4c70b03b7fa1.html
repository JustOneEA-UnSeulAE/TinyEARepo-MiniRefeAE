P14: Timely and Reliable Service Delivery ()
Principle 14: Timely and Reliable Service Delivery Statement: BDM will ensure that critical Government of Canada services are continuously available where possible and necessary, enabling timely and uninterrupted service delivery to Canadians. Rationale: Canadians and Government of Canada employees access services at all times and from multiple locations and expect them to be reliably available and responsive. Service demand can change rapidly, and digital services must scale efficiently while remaining operationally resilient. Ensuring high availability, secure access, and sustainable operations is essential to maintaining public trust and supporting uninterrupted government service delivery. ​ Implications: • Mission-Critical services will support 24/7 availability and response times will meet user and business needs.​ • Non-Mission-Critical services will support availability to the level that they require and strive for high availability where necessary and cost-effective.​ • Applications will remain accessible from any location using secure authentication.​ • Zero-downtime deployments for both planned and unplanned maintenance will be supported where possible and cost-effective.​ • Architecture & systems will be designed under the assumption that failures will happen and strive to handle errors gracefully.​ • Systems will be designed to allow performance and behaviour to be monitored actively.​ • Architectures will be designed to support new technology insertion with minimal disruption to existing programs and services.
