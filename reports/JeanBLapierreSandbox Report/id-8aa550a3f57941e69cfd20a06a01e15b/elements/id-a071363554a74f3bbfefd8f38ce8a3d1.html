Step 3: Data Quality & Lineage Validation Data Quality and Lineage Validation represent the second most critical component of the data certification process. This phase ensures that data assets meet established quality standards and that their lineage is thoroughly documented and verified. A comprehensive approach in this step is essential for creating a robust quality framework that supports further analysis, monitoring, and reporting. Data Quality Validation To assess the quality of data assets accurately, several dimensions of data quality must be evaluated: Accuracy: Verify that the data correctly represents the intended values and is free from error. Accurate data is essential for reliable reporting and decision-making. Completeness: Ensure that all required data elements are present and that no critical fields are missing. This dimension is vital for maintaining the integrity of the data. Timeliness/Freshness: Assess whether the data is current and relevant to business needs. Timeliness ensures that decisions are based on the most up-to-date information, while freshness addresses the data’s relevance and usability over time. Validity: Confirm that the data values conform to defined formats and business rules. Validity checks help in identifying erroneous or out-of-range values. Consistency: Ensure that data remains consistent across different datasets and reports, promoting trustworthiness and reliability. Uniqueness: Evaluate the data to ascertain that records are not duplicated unnecessarily, which can lead to skewed analysis and reporting. Integrity: Assess the integrity of the data assets across sources, ensuring that it maintains its intended structure and relationships, and is free from manipulation or corruption. Integrity checks help verify that the data has not been altered inappropriately during processing or storage. Lineage Validation After validating the quality of the data, the next step is to confirm the lineage of the data assets. This involves: End-to-End Lineage Confirmation: Validate that the entire data lineage - from the source through various transformations to the final output - has been accurately documented and verified. Understanding the data flow is critical for ensuring transparency and accountability. Manual Steps Explicitly Documented: All manual processes involved in the data flow must be clearly documented (as risk). This documentation serves as an audit trail that enhances trust in the data transformations. Steward Approval Required: Data Stewards play a vital role in this validation process. Their review and approval of the lineage documentation, transformations, and quality thresholds are essential for establishing an authoritative source of truth before proceeding to the next steps. In summary, Data Quality & Lineage Validation is integral to the data certification process. By conducting a thorough analysis of all reports, products, and underlying data assets, organizations can define relevant data quality dimensions and establish thresholds and pass/fail criteria for robust quality oversight. Validating lineage ensures that all aspects of data movement and transformation are accounted for and documented, providing valuable evidence for future reference, audits, and governance reviews. Ultimately, the authoritative approval from data stewards reinforces the integrity of the data assets and prepares them for effective utilization in decision-making processes. To facilitate the execution of this step, a few templates are available, including Appendix 3 (Data Quality Checklist), which serves as a foundational tool to guide users in documenting data quality, and Appendix 4 (Lineage Verification and Data Asset Migration Report), which focuses on lineage verification and helps ensure that all necessary elements are addressed and organized.
