Certified Asset ()
Certified Asset A Certified Asset denotes individual data assets that have been validated and certified for their quality and suitability. These data assets serve as the foundational building blocks for data products, meaning they are used in creating reports, analyses, and other outputs. Certified Assets undergo a standardized certification process that typically involves predefined extraction, transformation, and loading (ETL) procedures, along with essential quality checks. Unlike Certified Products, Certified Assets do not involve complex transformations or detailed analytical processes, allowing for a more straightforward certification pathway. This level of certification assures users of the asset's reliability and readiness for use in various applications.
