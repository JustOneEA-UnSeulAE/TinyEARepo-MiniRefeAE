P13: Strive Towards Automation ()
Principle 13: Strive Towards Automation Statement: BDM will prioritize automating routine tasks where possible to reduce the workload on project teams.​ Rationale: Minimizing manual intervention and automating tasks where possible increases efficiency in time and costs, allowing project teams to focus their time on other priorities. Automated tasks are also less error-prone than manual tasks.​ Routine tasks are relatively easy to automate since they typically require less specific knowledge. Implications: • BDM will explore for opportunities to introduce automation into routine tasks where it is possible to do so with current tools and minimal effort.​ • Project teams are assigned to tasks that require more complex knowledge, while simple routine tasks are automated.​ • Teams will make themselves aware of the impacts and risks involved with automating tasks and have a plan in place for how to assure automated tasks are done correctly and with a desirable level of quality.
