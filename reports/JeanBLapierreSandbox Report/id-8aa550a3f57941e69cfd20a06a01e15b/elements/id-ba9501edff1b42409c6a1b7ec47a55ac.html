P4: Treat data as an asset ()
Principle 4: Treat data as an asset​ Statement: BDM solutions and stakeholders have the data they need, which is fit for use, discoverable, and available. The use of this asset is appropriate, ethical, and generates social and economic value. Treating data as an asset enables better outcomes for citizens. Rationale: BDM must treat data as an asset: * Government of Canada Enterprise Architecture Framework - Canada.ca (https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/policies-standards/government-canada-enterprise-architecture-framework.html) To ensure products and services have the data they need To support and inspire greater use and availability of data To ensure data use is appropriate, citizen- and business-centric, and ethical To ensure data is well managed, secure, and fit for use To unlock future potential by combining with other sources to serve citizens better Implications: • BDM will strive to enable data driven approach to decision making to benefit clients. • BDM will enable data-as-a-service approach by enabling access to authoritative sources of data whenever possible. • Transferring and replicating data will be done judiciously and only when necessary for functional or performance reasons. • Data Architecture deliverables will be endorsed by the appropriate architecture governing bodies.
