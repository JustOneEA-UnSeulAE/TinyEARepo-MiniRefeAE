P10: Cloud Smart ()
Principle 10: Cloud Smart​ Statement: BDM will leverage cloud services providers & cloud-native services where appropriate and available. Rationale: • Optimizing use of cloud services maximizes business value, reduces accumulated technical debt, and continues to evolve the service-focused culture of ESDC. Most cloud providers also offer a rich set of managed services that provide functionalities to relieve the work of managing backend software or infrastructure. Leveraging cloud service providers and the managed services they offer instead of procuring or developing them provides opportunities for huge savings in time and operational overhead. Implications: • BDM will leverage cloud where applicable, available and where it meets BDM security requirements.​ • All cloud deployment options (including SaaS) will be explored and evaluated as part of the solution design.​ • BDM will ensure that requirements for performance, availability, and both horizontal and vertical scalability (including peak traffic periods) are met by all BDM solutions and components (new and legacy) and the hosting infrastructure.​ • Roles and responsibilities, governance, methods, skills, finances, and culture must shift to fully yield the agility of cloud services​ • The model will incentivize the continuous optimization of cloud consumption and provide agility to pivot to new technology choices.​ • Cloud native services will be leveraged where advantageous, balancing ease of use with portability concerns, and ensuring alignment with project goals and timelines.​ • Vendor lock-in will be mitigated.​
