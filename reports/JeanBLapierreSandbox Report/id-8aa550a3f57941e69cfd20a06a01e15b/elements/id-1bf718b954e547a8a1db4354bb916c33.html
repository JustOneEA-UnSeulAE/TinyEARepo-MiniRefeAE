Validated Data Asset ()
Validated Data Asset Validated Data Assets are those that have undergone the certification process but do not meet the criteria required for full certification. These assets may have been evaluated for quality, but certain aspects - such as completeness, accuracy, or compliance - may not align with certification standards. Additionally, Validated Data Assets may lack critical elements that could affect their usability and reliability: • Basic description: These assets may not have comprehensive descriptions outlining their purpose, scope, or content, which can lead to confusion about their intended use. • Owners assigned: Validated Data Assets may not have designated owners responsible for managing and maintaining the assets, leading to potential accountability gaps. • Sensitivity classification not identified and applied: Without appropriate sensitivity classifications, it becomes difficult to understand data protection requirements and compliance obligations, which may expose the organization to risks. • Glossary terms not defined and linked: Key terminologies associated with the data asset may not yet be defined or linked to a glossary, which can hinder effective communication and understanding among users. While they are not classified as Certified Assets, Validated Data Assets have still gone through essential quality checks and processes, offering some degree of assurance to users. These assets may be useful for exploratory analyses or ad-hoc reporting, but should be approached with caution, as they may not be suitable for high-stakes decision-making without further validation. The establishment of these certification levels and risk tiering allows organizations to articulate the varying degrees of quality, suitability, and risk associated with their data outputs. By clearly differentiating Certified Products, Certified Assets, and Validated Data Assets, organizations can effectively manage data governance and ensure that stakeholders understand the reliability and intended usage of each type of data asset. For further context, this document primarily focuses on Product/Report Certification; however, Appendix 7- Asset Certification Checklist (Purview Aligned) provides a checklist outlining the steps required to execute Asset Certification, with an emphasis on using Microsoft Purview as a tool for end-to-end process delivery.
