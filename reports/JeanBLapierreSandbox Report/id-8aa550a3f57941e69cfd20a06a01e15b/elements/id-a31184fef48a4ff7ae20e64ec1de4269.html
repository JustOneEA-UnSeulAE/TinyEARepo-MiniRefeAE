P7: Privacy by design ()
Principle 7: Privacy by design Statement: Respect client’s rights to privacy and identify and address privacy requirements early and throughout the service and solution design process. Rationale: Protection of clients’ personal information is required for any business process and corresponding IT solution that collects, stores and leverages client personal information. To ensure the protection of privacy without impeding or delaying service delivery privacy, requirements need to be addressed early in the solutioning process. Also, we need to be able to track privacy requirements back to business needs and to ensure that how service and solution designs meet the requirements. Implications: Collecting, processing, storing and sharing clients’ personal information is designed based on the lawful basis and authorities for doing so. • Privacy considerations are key to legislation, operational policy, service and business rules design. Through architecture and solution design, BDM will ensure that their intent regarding privacy is maintained in implementation and privacy risks minimized. • BDM will seek to reduce constraints related to data collection and data access and sharing, while maintaining a focus on privacy. • Consideration will be given to the full lifecycle of information management within a system and how that can affect privacy concerns.
