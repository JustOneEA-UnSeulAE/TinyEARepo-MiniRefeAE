Health benefits to Indigenous communities The spring economic update also allocates some new funding to Indigenous health initiatives. Namely, it earmarks $794 million this year to support the Non-Insured Health Benefits Program, “which provides First Nations and Inuit with coverage for a range of health products and services such as medical travel, pharmaceuticals, and mental health counselling.”
