P8: Security by design ()
Principle 8: Security by design​ Statement: BDM will embed security into all business practices and IT solutions from the outset, ensuring the protection of people and enterprise assets. This includes securing development pipelines and third-party components, with supply chain trust informing architectural decisions.​​ Rationale: Proactively integrating confidentiality, integrity, and availability into IM/IT solutions strengthens public trust, reduces risk, and improves resilience. This includes securing systems and the tools, services, and components used to build and deliver them. Evaluating the origin and trustworthiness of third-party suppliers, especially those from high-risk jurisdictions, helps prevent compromise. Supply chain vulnerabilities can emerge at any stage, so early risk assessments and secure design practices are essential to avoid costly retrofits and support obligations like monitoring, access control, and reporting.​ Implications: BDM will appropriately safeguard personal information in accordance with privacy legislation, while also delivering services consistent with the principles of digital government.​ • Security considerations will span the full lifecycle of information management within a system.​ • Security monitoring and reporting must be performed as an ongoing function, not one-time effort, and must be integral part of the operations.​ • Least privilege and privileged access control will be implemented from the start, in alignment with mandatory procedures outlined in the Directive on Security Management.​ • BDM will consider the origin, trustworthiness, and potential risks with third-party components and services, especially when sourced from jurisdictions that may pose national security concerns.​ • Lifecycle Integration: Security controls from ITSG-33 (e.g., SA-12 (https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a315sa12), SA-13 (https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a315sa13), SA-19 (https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/annex-3a-security-control-catalogue-itsg-33#a315sa19)) should be thoughtfully integrated across the system lifecycle, based on context and risk.
