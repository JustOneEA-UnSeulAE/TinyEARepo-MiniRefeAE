Recommended EARB Expected Decision Statement All initiatives presented to EARB must demonstrate one of the following: 1. Alignment with the Enterprise Data Reference Model (EDRM); 2. Completion of an EDRM mapping exercise identifying alignments and variances; or 3. A documented and supportable rationale explaining why EDRM alignment is not required. The assessment must include sufficient evidence to support enterprise data governance, interoperability, stewardship, and strategic data management objectives. [ESDC1_2023...2025-10-22 | PDF], [ESDC-CDOB...re - GUIDE | PDF] This structure should fit naturally alongside the existing EARB Data Alignment scoring methodology and could be introduced as either a new Data Architecture Alignment criterion within the Data Score or as a standalone mandatory EARB gate assessment.
