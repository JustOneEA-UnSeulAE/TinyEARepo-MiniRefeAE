Purpose of This Document
KEY CERTIFICATION BENEFITS
Defensible evidence for audit and regulators
Accountability for owners
A repeatable decision framework
Confidence for consumers
Establish trust in enterprise data assets by certifying they are fit for purpose, governed, and safe to consume [Objective]
WHAT IS CERTIFICATION?
Business-backed decision
Risk-aware and proportional
Evidence-based and time-bound
Repeatable and auditable
DATA CERTIFICATION LIMITATIONS
Not a one-time activity
Not a replacement for business judgment
Not a waiver of operational responsibility
Not a guarantee of perfect data
SCOPE & APPLICABILITY
Certified analytical, reporting, and regulatory datasets [In Scope]
Exploratory, experimental or temporary datasets [Out of Scope]
Ad-hoc analysis not shared broadly [Out of Scope]
Enterprise and domain-level data products [In Scope]
CERTIFICATION PRINCIPLES
Lifecycle-oriented
Business-led
Evidence-driven
Risk-based
Automation-first
