GC AI Compute Access Fund Program ()
https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/canadian-sovereign-ai-compute-strategy/ai-compute-access-fund/program-guide-ai-compute-access-fund
A. Accelerate Canadian AI innovation and commercialization by supporting the costliest component in the AI innovation value chain – compute [Objective]
B. Target support to Canadian AI SMEs engaging in research and development (R&D)/compute projects with a concrete pathway to commercialization [Objective]
C. Support use of a diverse range of external compute suppliers so companies can stay competitive in a rapidly changing industry [Objective]
Cover two-thirds of your eligible costs for Canadian, cloud-based compute services (2:1)
Cover half of your eligible costs for non-Canadian, cloud-based compute services (1:1)
